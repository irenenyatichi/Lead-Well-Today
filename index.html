WHO WE ARE

Leadership responsibility is growing faster than leadership capacity.

Lead Well Today exists to close that gap by developing high-impact, practical servant leaders equipped with the skills, character and mindset to lead effectively and drive societal transformation.

OUR VALUES

The LEAD framework.

Four convictions that shape how we develop people who can lead with influence, integrity and lasting impact.

L

Leadership

Taking responsibility and using influence to serve, equip and move others forward.

E

Ethics

Choosing integrity, accountability and principled action even when no one is watching.

A

Authenticity

Leading from a place of self-awareness, character and genuine conviction.

D

Distinction

Pursuing excellence and meaningful impact in every sphere of leadership.

OUR PROGRAMS

Leadership development
for where you are.

From pastors and emerging leaders to executives and career professionals, Lead Well creates practical spaces to grow the person behind the position.

A focused leadership immersion for pastors and ministry leaders who want to strengthen their self-leadership, leadership posture, vision and ability to develop others.

The Leadwell Pastors & Church Leaders Immersion creates space for pastors and ministry leaders to invest in themselves and strengthen the leadership capacity required for their next season.

An intensive 6-week course delivered digitally with expert-taught content and assignments to sharpen your leadership competence.

For leaders who cannot commit to a year-long experience, the Online Executive Accelerator delivers concentrated leadership development in six weeks. Expert-led content and practical assignments will help you sharpen how you lead.

A 12-month journey of highly personal, intentional and transformational leadership training.

This executive lab is crafted for leaders in corporate, marketplace, non-profit, church and ministry settings. It focuses on personal, team and organizational leadership to grow capacity and maximize effectiveness. The exclusive 4R Leadership Tool and Personal Transformation Project give participants a practical framework to assess leadership and apply learning for measurable impact.

For career-minded professionals who want to build the strategic leadership capacity needed to navigate their next level.

Tailor-made for career-minded individuals, this experience strengthens strategic leadership capacity and equips you to optimize career growth while learning to lead effectively across generations.

Join the 12-month Leadwell Leadership Experience and become part of a group of leaders committed to transforming communities and their organizations.

The experience is highly personalized and meets once monthly. It includes practical in-person training, group coaching, two leadership assessment tools and a personal transformation project that will impact your engagement with your church, community or organization as you apply your learning.

A 12-month cohort experience helping Christian leaders in their twenties develop the foundations to lead themselves, lead others and create meaningful impact.

This leadership journey is designed for young leaders already leading a ministry, team, business, initiative or community, as well as those preparing for greater responsibility. It provides the foundation, formation and practical experience needed to lead with greater clarity, confidence and impact.
